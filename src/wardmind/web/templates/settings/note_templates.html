Your note templates

A template is starting text for a note: the headings you fill in after a call, a meeting or a review. On an account's note form, choose one and its text fills the box. Nothing is saved until you add the note.

A template can use these words, which are replaced with the account's details when it's used: {account} (the account's name), {today} (today's date), {renewal_date} (the renewal date, or "no renewal date"), {owner} (the owner, if there is one) and {arr} (the ARR). Anything else in braces is kept as typed.
