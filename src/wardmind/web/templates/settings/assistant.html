Suggestions

Wardmind checks your book once a day and suggests next steps for you to accept or dismiss. Nothing changes until you accept one.

A kind of suggestion you keep dismissing as Not useful or Wrong is shown less. Health drops and renewals are always shown.

What Claude may change

Claude reads your book through Wardmind's MCP server. It never deletes or edits anything, and every change it makes is in the Activity log.

Claude's changes

Claude can read your book but can't change it. Its write tools say how to turn them on.

Claude can suggest a note, a follow-up or a snooze. Each one waits on Today until you accept it.

Claude can also add notes and follow-ups, mark a follow-up done and snooze an account. It shows a preview first and makes the change only when it is asked to confirm.
