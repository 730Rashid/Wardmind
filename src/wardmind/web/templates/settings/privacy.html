Web research

{% if web_on %}Web research is on.{% else %}Web research is off.{% endif %}

When it's on, each account page gets a Look up their website button. Pressing it visits that account's own website (its Website field), the way your browser would, and reads three things: the home page title, its description, and the news or blog feed the site publishes.

What is sent
Only an ordinary visit to the customer's website. Nothing about the account — ARR, owner, health, signals, notes — and nothing about your other accounts. No cookies are kept.
Who sees it
Only that customer's website, which sees a visit from your internet address, as with any visit. No search engine or other company is involved.
When
Only when you press the button, for that one account. Never in the background.
What is kept
The result, on this computer, for 24 hours. Clear local cache removes it, and so does turning web research off.
Demo data
Demo accounts are invented, so they show sample news and nothing is sent.
Safety
Wardmind won't visit an address on this computer or your private network, even if an imported website points there.

AI summaries

{{ ai_status }}

When it's on, Wardmind can turn what web research read from a customer's website into a short About and up to three highlights a CSM should know: acquisitions, funding, leadership changes or restructuring. It uses your own OpenRouter account, so you choose the model and pay OpenRouter directly.

What is sent
Only the text web research read from the customer's public website: its address, title, description and recent headlines. Nothing from your book: not your account name, ARR, owner, health, signals, notes or other accounts.
Who sees it
OpenRouter, and the company that runs the model you chose. Wardmind asks OpenRouter to use only providers that don't store or train on the data.
Cost
A summary is a few thousand tokens. With the default model, a book of 40 accounts usually costs about a cent or two. A summary is reused until the website or the model changes, so running it again only pays for what changed.
When
Only when you press Summarise on an account, or tick Also summarise with AI when looking up all websites. Never in the background.
What is kept
The summaries, on this computer, until the website or model changes. Clear local cache removes them, and so does turning AI summaries off.
Demo data
Demo accounts get invented sample summaries, and nothing is sent.

Local cache

Wardmind saves some things only to save time: website lookups, AI summaries and imports you started but didn't finish. Clearing them is safe. It doesn't touch your connections, imported or hand-added accounts, edits or settings.
